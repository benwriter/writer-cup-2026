# V4.1 Changelog

## Visual refresh
- Reworked the app into a light Writer Cup theme.
- Warm white/off-white background.
- Navy sampled from the Writer Cup logo for headings, buttons and primary text.
- Gold sampled from the logo for Cup accents and special-event highlights.
- Golf green used for live states, active selections and positive match information.
- Cards, navigation, course guide, weather, profiles and scoring screens updated for the new palette.

## Scoring usability
- Added **Clear Saved Scores** to any hole that already has a saved score.
- Clearing is scorer-PIN protected and requires confirmation.
- Clearing removes that hole from the live match/Cup calculation and restores blank score fields.
- NTP and Longest Drive results are deliberately not removed by clearing golf scores.
- Fixed the side-competition "No winner" payload to use a proper null winner value.

## Player photos
- Large player-profile photos can now be tapped to open full-screen.
- Full-screen viewer closes with the X button, by tapping outside the image, or by pressing Escape on a keyboard.

### Navigation polish
- Live dot now uses Writer Cup green.
- Score pencil now uses Writer Cup gold.
- Home trophy and Course golf icon remain unchanged.


## V4.1.2
- Score tab is now public and read-only by default.
- Spectators can browse hole scores, Stableford previews, match results and side-competition results without the scorer PIN.
- Scorer PIN unlocks editing for the current browser session.
- Backend score writes remain PIN protected.
- Player profile notes return to per-phone ownership: only the player selected under “This phone belongs to” can edit that player’s notes. Everyone can read all notes.
- Score pencil icon slightly enlarged and retained in gold.

## V4.1.3
- Player profiles now show full names: Ben Writer, Joel Ryan, Dylan Allen and Brent Rogers.
- Initials fallback updated to BW, JR, DA and BR.
- Hole 14 now has a scorer-controlled live tee-order randomiser that draws all four positions, highlights who tees off first, syncs the order through Supabase, and shows the same order in the Course Guide and scoring screen.
- Profile photos continue to replace initials automatically once uploaded; initials remain the fallback only.
