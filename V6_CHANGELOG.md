# V6 Changelog

## Major format rebuild
- Holes 1–6 changed from Foursomes to Writer Cup Scramble.
- Holes 7–12 changed to Four-Ball Combined Team Stableford.
- Holes 13–18 changed to Aggregate Singles Stableford.
- Singles pairings remain Ben v Dylan and Joel v Brent.
- Cup remains four total points.

## Rules / presentation
- Added light-shirt / dark-shirt team requirement.
- Added Coast Local Rules summary and direct official link.
- Added Competition Director ruling instruction.
- Removed “White Tees” from the Home hero.
- Home countdown now targets 7:00am tee time.
- Added Sponsors & Partners page under More.

## Scoring UI
- Stableford previews now appear on Holes 7–18.
- Four-Ball result cards show combined team Stableford totals.
- Singles Live cards show running six-hole aggregate Stableford totals.
- Player profile stats now show accumulated Stableford points instead of Singles hole wins.

## V6 scoring reliability pass
- Clear Saved Scores now targets the hole currently displayed in the scorer, even when the official live hole has already advanced.
- After saving, the scorer stays on the saved hole so it can be reviewed or cleared immediately; use the right arrow to move on.
- Stableford shots received are explicitly displayed for every player on both Holes 7–12 and Holes 13–18.
- Scramble teeing order now alternates between teams, e.g. Joel, Dylan, Ben, Brent.
- Correct sponsor spellings confirmed: Shell Petroleum and Tri-Lite Golf Buggies.
